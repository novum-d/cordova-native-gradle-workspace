Kotlin standard-library third-party code
======================================

The Kotlin 1.9.24 upstream license/README.md identifies:

- libraries/stdlib/src/kotlin/collections: derived from GWT,
  (C) 2007-08 Google Inc.; Apache License 2.0.
- libraries/stdlib/unsigned/src/kotlin/UnsignedUtils.kt: derived from
  Guava's UnsignedLongs, (C) 2011 The Guava Authors; Apache License 2.0.
- libraries/stdlib/jvm/src/kotlin/util/MathJVM.kt: derived from Boost
  special math functions, Copyright Eric Ford & Hubert Holin 2001;
  Boost Software License 1.0.

The upstream license texts are retained in kotlin-gwt-LICENSE.txt,
kotlin-guava-LICENSE.txt and kotlin-boost-LICENSE.txt. The full upstream
licensing README is retained as source evidence, not as a statement that
the Kotlin compiler, JS/Native/Wasm libraries or its test data are shipped.
